Who it's for

Businesses that don't have an IT department, or whose IT person is stretched too thin. If your team loses time to slow computers, password lockouts or software that won't cooperate, managed IT takes that burden away for one predictable monthly fee.

Every request goes into our ticketing system, so nothing gets lost and you can see what's happening. Most issues are solved remotely within minutes, and we come on site when needed. Behind the scenes, AI-powered monitoring watches your systems around the clock and fixes routine problems before anyone has to call.

Questions about managed IT services and 24/7/365 support

Do you really offer support 24/7/365?

Yes. Ku Tech's help desk is available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, including weekends and holidays.

What does Level 1-3 support mean?

Level 1 covers everyday requests like password resets. Level 2 handles deeper troubleshooting of devices and software. Level 3 covers complex server, network and cloud problems that need senior engineers. Ku Tech covers all three.
