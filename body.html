Common questions

What is a managed service provider?

A managed service provider (MSP) takes ongoing responsibility for your IT for a monthly fee. Instead of calling someone only when things break, you get a team that monitors, maintains and secures your technology all the time.

When can I get support?

Any time. Ku Tech provides 24/7/365 support, and every request is logged in our ticketing system so nothing gets lost. Many issues are fixed remotely within minutes, and we come on site when needed.

How does Ku Tech use AI?

AI watches your systems continuously, spots patterns that point to trouble and runs routine fixes automatically. Our engineers review what it finds and handle anything that needs a human decision, so issues are resolved faster and many never reach your team.

We already have an IT person. Can you still help?

Yes. With co-managed IT, we work alongside your in-house staff, adding monitoring, security tools, extra hands and specialist skills while they stay in charge.

Can you help with a one-time project?

Yes. Alongside ongoing managed services, we take on projects such as cloud migrations, office moves, datacenter installations and technology audits, with a timeline built around your operations.

Is there a long-term contract?

We'll walk you through the agreement options during your assessment and pick the one that fits your business. Our goal is to earn your business every month.

How long does it take to switch to Ku Tech?

It depends on the size of your setup. We agree a timeline up front, coordinate with your current provider and keep your team informed at every step.

Not sure what you need?

Tell us what's going on and we'll recommend the right mix of services. No pressure and no jargon.
