Questions about datacenter and infrastructure services

What is a UPS and do I need one?

A UPS (uninterruptible power supply) keeps servers and network equipment running through short power cuts and lets them shut down safely in longer ones. Most businesses with on-site servers need one.

Can you help with an office move?

Yes. We plan and install networking, Wi-Fi, phones and equipment at the new site so your team is ready to work on day one.
