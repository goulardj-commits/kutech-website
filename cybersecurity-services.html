Who it's for

Every business that handles customer data, payments or sensitive files, which is nearly all of them. Attackers increasingly target smaller companies because they expect weaker defenses.

How Ku Tech delivers it

We start by finding your biggest risks, then put protections in place across devices, email, networks and applications. Continuous monitoring and regular testing keep those defenses current, security awareness training helps your team spot phishing, and a clear response plan means that if something does happen, we contain it and get you back to normal quickly.

Questions about cybersecurity and response management

Is my business too small to be a target?

No. Small and mid-sized businesses are frequent targets because attackers expect them to have fewer protections.

What happens if we are attacked?

Ku Tech's incident response contains the threat, restores affected systems from clean backups and helps you understand what happened and how to prevent it.
