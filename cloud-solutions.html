Cloud solutions and Microsoft 365

Ku Tech helps businesses move to the cloud and get the most from it. We assess which systems belong in the cloud, plan and carry out the migration, train your team and manage Microsoft 365, Google Workspace and cloud servers from then on, so you can work securely from anywhere.

What's included

  • Cloud assessment and analysis
  • Migration planning and training
  • Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace
  • Azure and cloud server management
  • File sharing and collaboration

Who it's for

Businesses with aging on-site servers, teams that work from several locations, or anyone paying for cloud licenses they don't fully use.

How Ku Tech delivers it

We begin with a cloud assessment that weighs cost, security and performance. Then we plan the migration around your schedule to avoid downtime, move your email, files and applications, and train your people on the new tools. After the move, we manage accounts, licenses and security settings as part of your plan.

Questions about cloud solutions and Microsoft 365

Will moving to the cloud disrupt my business?

Migrations are planned around your operations and are usually done outside business hours, so your team can keep working.

Can you help us use Microsoft 365 better?

Yes. Many businesses pay for Microsoft 365 features they never set up. We configure and train your team on the tools that fit how you work.
