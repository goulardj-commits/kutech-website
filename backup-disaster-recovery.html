Backup and disaster recovery

Ku Tech's backup and disaster recovery service makes sure your data is always protected and can be restored when it matters. We run automated, encrypted backups, back up Microsoft 365, test restores regularly and build a continuity plan so your business can recover quickly from hardware failure, mistakes or cyberattacks.

What's included

  • Automated, encrypted backups
  • Microsoft 365 backup
  • Regular restore testing

Who it's for

Any business that couldn't afford to lose its files, email or customer records, or to be offline for days while systems are rebuilt.

How Ku Tech delivers it

Backups run automatically and are monitored so a failed job is caught and re-run right away. We regularly test restores to prove your data can actually come back, and we document a recovery plan that sets out what gets restored first and how quickly.

Questions about backup and disaster recovery

Doesn't Microsoft 365 already back up my data?

Microsoft keeps its service running, but it's your responsibility to protect your data against deletion, ransomware and mistakes. A separate Microsoft 365 backup covers that gap.

How do I know my backups work?

Ku Tech tests restores on a regular schedule and reports the results, so you're not relying on hope.
