Mobile workforce management

Ku Tech's mobile workforce management keeps teams secure and productive wherever they work. We analyze your mobile providers and support migrations, set up and manage phones and laptops, and secure every device and endpoint so company data stays protected at home, on the road and in the office.

What's included

  • Provider analysis and migration support
  • Device and endpoint security for mobile staff
  • Mobile device setup and management
  • Secure access to company data on the go

Who it's for

Businesses with remote or hybrid staff, field teams, or employees who use phones and laptops to reach company email and files.

How Ku Tech delivers it

We review your mobile plans and providers to find the best fit, then manage devices centrally so they're set up consistently, kept updated and protected. If a device is lost or stolen, company data can be locked or wiped remotely.

Questions about mobile workforce management

Can you secure personal phones that staff use for work?

Yes. Mobile device management can protect company email and files on personal devices without touching personal data.

Can you help us switch mobile providers?

Yes. We compare providers and plans and support the migration so your team stays connected.
