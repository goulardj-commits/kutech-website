AI-powered IT monitoring and automation

Ku Tech uses AI-powered monitoring and automation to keep your business up and running. It watches your servers, devices, network and cloud accounts around the clock, spots warning signs early and fixes routine issues automatically, while our engineers handle anything that needs a human decision.

What's included

  • Around-the-clock system monitoring
  • Predictive alerts before failures
  • Automated fixes for common issues
  • Performance and capacity tracking
  • Clear monthly health reports
  • Engineers on hand for anything complex

Who it's for

Any business where downtime costs money: missed sales, idle staff or unhappy customers. It's especially valuable if you've been living with the break-fix cycle of calling someone only after something stops working.

How Ku Tech delivers it

Our monitoring detects unusual behavior, diagnoses the likely cause in seconds and runs proven fixes automatically, such as freeing disk space, restarting stuck services or re-running failed updates. Bigger issues go straight to a Ku Tech engineer with the full picture, and every incident teaches the system how to prevent the next one.

Questions about AI-powered IT monitoring and automation

Does AI replace your engineers?

No. AI handles the constant watching and routine fixes. Ku Tech engineers review what it finds, make the decisions and handle anything complex.

Will I see what the monitoring finds?

Yes. You get clear reports on the health of your systems and what was fixed, in plain language.
