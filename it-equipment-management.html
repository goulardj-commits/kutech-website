IT equipment management

Ku Tech's IT equipment management makes sure the right hardware is in the right hands. We handle equipment purchasing and support, track every device, analyze and streamline how new equipment is deployed, and manage vendors, so you spend less time on hardware and less money on the wrong gear.

What's included

Who it's for

Growing businesses that buy computers and devices regularly, or anyone who isn't sure what equipment they own, where it is or when it's due for replacement.

How Ku Tech delivers it

We recommend and buy equipment that fits your needs and budget, set it up to your standards and track it from purchase to retirement. A defined deployment process means new hires get working devices on day one, and we deal with vendors and warranties for you.

Questions about IT equipment management

Can you buy equipment for us?

Yes. Ku Tech sources and purchases equipment on your behalf, then configures it before it reaches your staff.

Why does asset tracking matter?

Knowing what you own helps you plan replacements, control costs and keep lost or old devices from becoming security risks.
